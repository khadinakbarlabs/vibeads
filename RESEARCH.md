## Product choices

VibeAds joins approved public Actor records to source-linked decisions rather than including an ad-account management server. Ten focused skills share one evidence/cost protocol. It separates curated TikTok Top Ads from European library records, current query-sponsored Google ads from Transparency creatives, crawler metadata from conversion tests, and event estimates from platform/child bills. The plugin generates original briefs and conditional budgets, not media or ad-account changes.

## Verification boundaries

Public Actor metadata/build contracts were inspected for ten publisher-owned Actors. Default build pins are explicit. Actor contracts and example inputs do not demonstrate paid runtime output, provider credits, native-host effectiveness, security-scan results or directory approval. Those evidence states remain separate. The live Anthropic CLI/MCP acknowledgement issue is recorded in SUBMISSION.md.
